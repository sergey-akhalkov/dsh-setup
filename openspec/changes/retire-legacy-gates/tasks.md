# Tasks

The migration has one serial order: switches and parity first, then one gate at a time. The tracked parity report is the gate for every flip. Lane 1 owns the two provider switches; lane 2 owns the parity harness and report; lane 3 owns the workflow definitions and their tests; lane 5 is the serial setup/docs/release tail. The spec-review, task-progress, and lane-assignment/lane-boundary gates are retained and must not be flipped in this change, and the owned task-review gate must reproduce the completion coverage for foreign tasks.

## Workflow follow-up

- Run the parity suite on the owner machine, commit the tracked report, flip one gate at a time in a throwaway profile, and confirm one task-review and checkpoint steer while the spec-review, progress, and lane gates still steer for their cases.
- Release the switches, the tracked report, and the `own` definitions; keep the legacy rows installed and disabled for one release, keep the spec-review rule enabled, and keep the retained progress and lane gates enabled.
- After one release and a live profile check, delete the dead legacy listeners and the verification-gate row; keep the `work_checkpoint`, `task_progress`, review, queue, the spec-review rule, and the retained progress and lane gates.
- A later change must add the workflow obligation that preserves the spec-review, reviewed-tick-or-current-progress-note, stale-note, single-remaining-lane, and below-checkpoint-threshold cases, pass the release-bound parity, and only then disable the retained legacy gates.
- Archive the change after the project's review requirements are satisfied; record the parity report date, the template hashes, the settings matrix, and the live result.
